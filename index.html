Who is Sajid Islam?

Sajid Islam is a Computer Science undergraduate at FAST National University of Computing and Emerging Sciences (NUCES) in Pakistan, studying from August 2024 with an expected graduation in May 2028. He works as an AI engineer on agentic AI and LLM applications, and is based in Peshawar, Pakistan.

What does Sajid build?

He builds agentic AI systems and the backends that support them: LLM-orchestrated retrieval pipelines, tool-using agents, and Python APIs. His most complete project is the WhatsApp Commerce Copilot, an AI copilot that answers a clothing brand's WhatsApp customers from its own catalogue, built by a four-person squad in Comebck Pakistan Cohort 1. His résumé records it as a Top 5 project of that cohort.

What agentic AI and RAG experience does he have?

In the WhatsApp Commerce Copilot, inbound messages pass through a LangChain intent chain, and generation is restricted to ambiguous queries and grounded in store-scoped products and policies retrieved by the backend rather than open model knowledge. He also completed the GIKI × Skylabs Advanced AI Bootcamp, a two-month intensive covering agentic AI with LangChain and RAG alongside deep learning, NLP and MLOps tooling.

Which programming and backend technologies does he use?

Python is his primary language, with FastAPI for services and LangChain plus the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs for LLM work. On the data side: PostgreSQL and PL/pgSQL, MySQL, MongoDB and ETL pipelines. He also works in C++, Java, PHP and JavaScript, and uses Docker, Git, DVC and MLflow for packaging and tracking.

Where can I read about how he builds things?

Two case studies cover the projects end to end — the WhatsApp Commerce Copilot and the Entropy-Aware Data Preservation System — each with an architecture diagram and an explicit limitations section. Longer technical notes are collected under writing, currently in draft.
